F1 Overtake Prediction - Data Dependencies & Conditions

1. Prediction Target Condition (Overtake_Success = 1)
-----------------------------------------------------
For the machine learning model to predict an overtake will happen, the following conditions must be met in the telemetry dataset during the lookahead window:
- Lookahead Window: ~5 seconds into the future (33 telemetry rows).
- Gap Threshold: The driver's gap to the car ahead must be under 1.0 second.
- Position Change: The driver's race position must improve within that 5-second window.

2. Input Features (Data used to make the prediction)
-----------------------------------------------------
The XGBoost model relies on the following 10 data points (features) to determine if the condition above will happen:
- Speed: Vehicle speed (km/h)
- Throttle: Throttle pedal pressure (0-100%)
- Brake: Brake pedal pressure (0-100%)
- RPM: Engine RPM
- DRS: Drag Reduction System activation status (0/1)
- Acceleration: Calculated acceleration/deceleration rate
- Estimated_SoC: Estimated State of Charge of the battery (%)
- Gap_to_Ahead: Time gap to the car directly in front (seconds)
- TyreLife: Number of laps completed on the current set of tires
- Compound_Encoded: Numerical encoding of the tire compound used (Soft, Medium, Hard, etc.)
