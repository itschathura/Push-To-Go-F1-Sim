Research Methodology

Four-stage pipeline connecting physics-informed battery estimation with machine learning

Stage 1
1

Data Ingestion & Preprocessing

  • FastF1 & OpenF1 APIs: Synchronised ~3.7 Hz telemetry feed across 12+ Grand Prix sessions.
  • Channel Filtering: Speed, Throttle, Brake pressure, Engine RPM, Gear, Car coordinates.
  • Event Annotation: Ground truth overtake tagging derived from official lap position change matrices.
FastF1 OpenF1 ~3.7 Hz Telemetry
Stage 2 · Novel Step
2

Physics-Informed SoC Estimation

  • 2026 PU Energy Balance: Integrates power flow based on FIA regulatory constraints.
  • Depletion Modeling: 350 kW MGU-K at 100% throttle = 8.75% / sec drain of 4 MJ capacity.
  • Regen Approximation: Kinetic recovery integration during brake events & super-clipping.
SoC(t) = SoC(0) + ∫ (P_regen - P_deploy) dt / E_battery
First Principles FIA 2026 Rules
Stage 3
3

Feature Engineering & ML Classifier

  • Relative Battle Dynamics: ΔSoC (chaser vs leader), gap-to-ahead, speed differential, longitudinal acceleration.
  • Strategic Context: Tyre compound delta & relative tyre age (stint wear).
  • XGBoost Model: Extreme Gradient Boosting binary classifier tuned for class-imbalanced overtake intervals.
XGBoost Scikit-learn SMOTE / Weights
Stage 4
4

Two-Layer System Architecture

  • Layer 1 (Offline Batch): Historical model training, feature importance extraction & artifact persistence.
  • Layer 2 (Live-Style Stream): High-throughput ingestion, real-time feature engine & ScyllaDB low-latency store.
  • Inference Serving: Sub-50ms overtake probability broadcasted to real-time UI.
ScyllaDB FastAPI React / Streamlit
